## Aesthetic Risk Taken

**The Risk:** Forest green + aged brass color palette

**Why it's risky:**
- Green can feel medical/corporate
- Brass can feel old-fashioned
- Not safe gray/white/blue

**Why it works:**
- Grounded in Japanese materials
- Aged brass = established, trustworthy
- Forest = calm, natural
- Distinctive in HK interior design market
