# Claude AI Agent Instructions

This document provides context for AI agents working on the Mori Living landing page project.

## Brand Context

**Mori Living** is a Hong Kong interior design and renovation studio with a clear identity:

### Design Philosophy
- **Japanese-inspired contemporary living**
- Minimalist aesthetic (think Muji, UNIQLO store design)
- Suited to compact Hong Kong homes (typically 400-700 sq ft)
- Natural materials, neutral palettes, functional beauty

### Brand Voice
- **Professional but approachable** - Not pretentious or overly luxurious
- **Calm and reassuring** - Renovation is stressful; we're the solution
- **Detail-oriented** - Japanese precision and craftsmanship
- **Honest** - Price transparency, realistic timelines

### Target Audience Psychographics

**Primary:** First-time renovators (anxious, overwhelmed)
- Just purchased their first property
- Afraid of contractor scams
- Want quality but need budget predictability
- Time-poor professionals (28-45)

**Secondary:** Second-home upgraders
- More confident but want design expertise
- Higher budgets (HK$800K-1.2M)
- Value aesthetics and functionality equally

## Hong Kong Market Context

### Critical Local Considerations
1. **Mobile-first** - 90%+ of Meta ad clicks will be mobile
2. **WhatsApp is king** - More trusted than email for initial contact
3. **Price sensitivity** - HK property prices are extreme; people need budget clarity
4. **Space constraints** - Every design must maximize small spaces
5. **Fast-paced** - HK users want quick information, clear next steps

## Conversion Goals

### Primary Goal
Get visitor to **book free consultation**

### Success Metrics
- Form submissions
- WhatsApp clicks
- Time on page >45 seconds

### Conversion Psychology
1. **Reduce anxiety** - Show completed projects, process clarity
2. **Build trust** - 100+ projects, testimonials, transparent pricing
3. **Lower friction** - Free consultation, no commitment

## Design System

### Colors
- **Primary:** Soft whites and off-whites (#FAFAFA, #F5F5F5)
- **Accent:** Sage green (#A8B89E) - natural, calming
- **Text:** Charcoal gray (#2D2D2D)
- **Secondary:** Warm wood tones (#D4A574)

### Typography
- **Headings:** Clean sans-serif (Inter, SF Pro)
- **Body:** Readable sans-serif, 16px minimum

### Layout Principles
- **Breathing room** - Japanese ma (negative space)
- **Mobile-first** - Design for 375px width first
- **Sticky CTA** - Keep "Book Consultation" always visible

## Key Messages
- "One dedicated designer, start to finish"
- "100+ Hong Kong homes completed"
- "Fixed-price quotations"
- "8-12 week typical timeline"
- "Free design consultation with no obligation"
