# Design Rationale - Mori Living Landing Page

## Design Brief
**Subject:** Interior design studio for compact Hong Kong homes  
**Audience:** Anxious first-time renovators (28-45, just bought property)  
**Job:** Convert Meta ad traffic into consultation bookings

## Design Plan

### Color Palette
```
#FDFCFB - Warm paper white (not the default #F4F1EA cream)
#2B3A2E - Deep forest green (not pure black)
#C8A882 - Aged brass (not terracotta)
#E8E4DC - Pale stone
#4A5D4F - Moss green accent
#1A1F1C - Charcoal ink
```

**Why these colors:**
- Derived from Japanese materials: paper, forest, brass, stone
- Avoids AI-generated defaults (cream + terracotta, black + acid green)
- Natural, calm palette addresses renovation anxiety
- Aged brass feels established, not startup

### Typography
- **Display:** Instrument Serif - delicate but grounded (not high-contrast serif)
- **Body:** Inter with looser tracking - breathable, Japanese ma principle
- **Scale:** 8xl / 5xl / 3xl / 2xl / lg / base

**Why this pairing:**
- Instrument Serif is distinctive but not showy
- Inter is familiar but tracking makes it feel spacious
- Not the default San Francisco + Playfair combo

### Layout Philosophy
**Not:** Broadsheet grid, stats on gradient, 01/02/03 markers

**Instead:** Vertical scroll story, like unrolling a Japanese scroll
- Hero: massive negative space, hooks pain point directly
- "Instead..." section: one real project photo as window
- Timeline: signature element that addresses time anxiety
- Everything else: quiet and disciplined

### Signature Element
**Timeline scrubber** (Week 1-12) that follows scroll progress.

**Why this works:**
- Directly addresses core anxiety: "how long will this take?"
- Makes time visible and manageable
- Interactive without being gimmicky
- Specific to renovation (not generic progress bar)

## Self-Critique Against Defaults

### ❌ What I Avoided (Generic AI Defaults)

1. **Hero with stats on gradient**
   - Template: Big number + small label + gradient accent
   - Why avoided: Every landing page does this

2. **Numbered markers (01 / 02 / 03)**
   - Template: Sequential numbers as decoration
   - Why avoided: Only appropriate if content is actually a sequence
   - Our timeline shows real weeks (1, 4, 8, 12) with meaning

3. **Cream background (#F4F1EA) + terracotta accent**
   - Template: Current AI-generated default look
   - Why avoided: Appears regardless of subject
   - Used warm paper + aged brass instead

4. **Generic copy ("Beautiful spaces", "Transform your home")**
   - Template: Vague aspirational language
   - Why avoided: Makes design feel templated
   - Used specific copy: "520 sq ft Sai Ying Pun"

### ✅ What Makes This Distinctive

1. **Pain-point hook: "Your renovation doesn't have to feel like this"**
   - Conversational, specific, addresses anxiety directly
   - Not "Transform your space" or "Design excellence"

2. **"Instead..." section**
   - One photo as window to calm space
   - Not gallery bombardment or hero slider
   - Caption is specific: location, size, outcome

3. **Timeline as interactive scroll element**
   - Progress bar shows weeks 1-12 as you scroll
   - Makes time feel manageable
   - Specific to this brief (renovation timeline anxiety)

4. **Restraint principle applied**
   - Timeline is the one bold element
   - Everything else is quiet: simple borders, ample space
   - No competing decorations

5. **Copy is specific, not generic**
   - "HK$580K" not "affordable"
   - "8-12 weeks" not "fast"
   - "520 sq ft Sai Ying Pun" not "beautiful studio"

## Typography Treatment

### Display Face (Instrument Serif)
- Large headlines: 96px / 80px / 64px
- Negative letter-spacing: -0.02em (tighter, more deliberate)
- Used sparingly: hero, section titles, testimonial quotes

### Body Face (Inter)
- Sizes: 20px / 18px / 16px / 14px
- Positive letter-spacing: 0.01em (breathable)
- Line height: 1.75 (breathing principle)

**Why this matters:**
- Type treatment is memorable part of design
- Not neutral delivery vehicle
- Breathing space reflects Japanese aesthetic

## Structure Encodes Information

### Timeline Week Numbers
```
Week 1  → Consultation
Week 2  → Design proposal
Week 4  → Construction starts
Week 8  → Midpoint check
Week 12 → Handover
```

**Why this structure:**
- Real timeline, not decoration
- Numbers encode actual weeks (1, 2, 4, 8, 12)
- Not generic 01 / 02 / 03 / 04 / 05

### Border-left markers
- Used only for benefits and timeline
- Encodes "this is a list of things in order"
- Not decorative lines everywhere

## Motion Strategy

### Orchestrated moments:
1. **Hero fade-in** - slow (1.2s), sets calm tone
2. **Timeline scrubber** - follows scroll, shows progress
3. **Testimonial reveals** - staggered, but subtle

### What I avoided:
- Scattered hover effects
- Parallax on every section
- Cursor trails, particles
- Anything that screams "AI-generated"

**One orchestrated moment** (timeline scrubber) lands harder than scattered effects.

## Writing in Design

### Active Voice Examples
- "Book consultation — it's free" (not "Submit")
- "We'll call you tomorrow" (not "Your request has been received")
- "One designer. Start to finish." (not "Full-service design solutions")

### Specific > Clever
- "520 sq ft Sai Ying Pun" not "Maximizing small spaces"
- "HK$580K" not "Affordable luxury"
- "8-12 weeks" not "Fast turnaround"

### Address Anxiety Directly
- "Your renovation doesn't have to feel like this"
- "No surprises, no change orders"
- "We tell you exactly what happens when"

## Restraint Applied

### Chanel's Mirror Principle
*"Before leaving the house, remove one accessory"*

**What I removed:**
- ❌ Sticky header (competing CTA)
- ❌ Multiple accent colors
- ❌ Icons/illustrations
- ❌ Photo gallery grid
- ❌ Stats module
- ❌ Team photos
- ❌ Partner logos

**What stayed:**
- ✅ Timeline scrubber (the one signature element)
- ✅ Border-left markers (encode structure)
- ✅ One "Instead..." photo section
- ✅ Simple forms, simple CTAs

**Result:** Boldness spent in one place. Everything else is disciplined.

## Critique Against Brief

### Does it address the audience?
✅ **Yes**
- Hooks anxiety: "doesn't have to feel like this"
- Shows timeline clearly (addresses "how long?")
- Fixed pricing (addresses "hidden costs?")
- Real testimonials (addresses "can I trust them?")

### Does it differentiate from competitors?
✅ **Yes**
- Not templated (avoided all three AI defaults)
- Color palette from Japanese materials
- Timeline scrubber is unique
- Copy is specific, not generic

### Does it convert?
✅ **Yes**
- Clear CTA: "Book consultation — it's free"
- Low friction: optional fields
- WhatsApp alternative (HK standard)
- Testimonials build trust

### Is it grounded in the subject?
✅ **Yes**
- Japanese aesthetic (ma principle, restraint)
- Hong Kong specifics (520 sq ft, district names)
- Renovation timeline (8-12 weeks)
- Real materials (paper, brass, stone)

## Final Check: Is This Distinctive?

**Test:** If I gave this same brief to myself again, would I arrive at the same design?

**Answer:** No. The specific choices are:
- Timeline scrubber (not stats module)
- "Doesn't have to feel like this" (not "Transform your space")
- Aged brass + forest green (not terracotta + cream)
- Instrument Serif (not Playfair or Inter Tight)
- One photo as window (not gallery grid)

**Verdict:** These are deliberate choices for this brief, not defaults.
